## scripts

- `01_tailocin_killing_matrix.R`  
  Plot phylogenetic tree + tailocin killing matrix heatmap.

- `02_tailocin_killing_summary.R`  
  Summarize killing assay results by OBC presence/absence; Wilcoxon tests.

- `03_plant_health_OBC.R`  
  Compare mean green pixels (plant health) between OBC groups.

- `04a_tnseq_plant.R`  
  Process raw in planta TnSeq read counts, map orthologs, and merge plant and tailocin fitness data.

- `04b_tnseq_scatter.R`  
  Visualize TnSeq-derived plant vs tailocin fitness trade-offs.

- `05_geneplot.R`  
  Plot gene map of the focal O-antigen biosynthesis cluster.

- `06_plant_infections_luciferase.R`  
  Analyze luciferase-based bacterial load at 7 dpi.

- `07_plant_CFUs.R`  
  Quantify in planta bacterial growth via CFU counts.

- `08_plant_disease.R`  
  Analyze qualitative plant disease outcomes.

- `09_disease_vs_load.R`  
  Relate plant health outcomes to bacterial load.

- `10_invitro_growth.R`  
  Analyze in vitro growth curves and estimate growth parameters.

- `11_aggregation.R`  
  Quantify aggregation phenotypes across strains.

- `12_AMP.R`  
  Analyze antimicrobial peptide (AMP) disk diffusion assays; calculate inhibition areas and perform dose–response statistics (Fig. S6).

- `13_oxidative_stress.R`  
  Quantify oxidative stress tolerance across H₂O₂ gradients.

- `14a_qPCR_processing_example.R`  
  Example pipeline for processing raw qPCR RFU data into MNE-normalized expression values.

- `14b_qPCR.R`  
  Aggregate and statistically analyze qPCR expression data across experiments.
