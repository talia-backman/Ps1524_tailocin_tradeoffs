# Figures

This folder contains output figures generated by the analysis scripts in `../scripts/`.
Each file corresponds to a figure panel or supplementary figure in the manuscript.
Figure numbering follows the manuscript order.

## Main Figures

- `01_tailocin_killing_matrix.pdf`  
  Tailocin killing matrix heatmap with phylogenetic tree and HTF-length annotations (Fig. 1A).

- `02_tailocin_killing_summary.pdf`  
  Proportion of sensitive vs resistant strains summarized by O-antigen biosynthesis cluster (OBC) presence (Fig. 1B).

- `03_plant_health_OBC.pdf`  
  Pixel-based plant health quantification comparing OBC-present and OBC-absent strains (Fig. 1C).

- `04b_tnseq_scatter.pdf`  
  Scatterplot of TnSeq-derived plant fitness vs tailocin fitness, highlighting resistance trade-offs (Fig. 2B).

- `05_geneplot.pdf`  
  Gene map of the focal O-antigen biosynthesis cluster colored by TnSeq results (Fig. 2C).

- `06_plant_infections_luciferase.pdf`  
  Luciferase-based bacterial load (fold-change at 7 dpi) across strains and host ecotypes (Fig. 3C).

## Supplementary Figures

- `07_plant_CFUs.pdf`  
  In planta bacterial growth quantified as CFU per leaf across ecotypes, doses, and timepoints (Fig. S1).

- `08_plant_disease.pdf`  
  Qualitative plant disease outcomes (healthy vs diseased/dead) by strain and ecotype (Fig. S2).

- `09_disease_vs_load.pdf`  
  Relationship between bacterial load and plant health outcomes (Fig. S3).

- `10_invitro_growth.pdf`  
  In vitro growth curves of wild-type and O-antigen mutants (Fig. S4).

- `11_aggregation.pdf`  
  Aggregation phenotype proportions across strains (Fig. S5).

- `12_AMP.pdf`  
  Antimicrobial peptide (AMP) disk diffusion assays showing inhibition area as a function of AMP concentration (Fig. S6).

- `13_oxidative_stress.pdf`  
  Growth responses of WT and O-antigen mutants across hydrogen peroxide gradients (Fig. S7).

- `14_qPCR.pdf`  
  Expression dynamics of Arabidopsis immune marker genes following infection with WT and O-antigen mutants (Fig. S8).
